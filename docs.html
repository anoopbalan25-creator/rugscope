How Rugscope works

Rugscope watches the page you are already viewing, finds the token address, shows a risk summary, and badges tracked wallet activity directly on the page. It is built to give context before you trade, not to tell you what to buy or sell.

What happens when you open a token page

  1. Rugscope scans the page URL, title, metadata, links, app data, page attributes, and visible text for token addresses.
  2. When it finds a likely token, it checks public market and risk data.
  3. A movable risk panel appears on the page with the score, warnings, facts, and tracked wallet trades.
  4. The panel starts expanded so you can read the result immediately. You can drag it, resize it, or collapse it when you want more screen space.

What Rugscope checks

Liquidity

Thin pools, very low liquidity, and sharp liquidity drops can make a token easier to move or drain.

Pair age

Very new pools are treated with more caution because there is less trading history to review.

Market pressure

Rugscope looks at price drops, buy/sell pressure, FDV compared with liquidity, and recent activity.

Creator risk

For Solana tokens, Rugscope checks authority, LP lock, holder concentration, and listed risk warnings.

Wallet activity

Tracked wallet buys and sells are matched against recent pool trades and surfaced in the panel and page markers.

Trade details

When a tracked wallet trade is found, Rugscope shows the side, amount, USD volume, time, and transaction link.

Wallet tracking

  1. Open a token page and wait for the Rugscope panel to appear.
  2. Paste a wallet address into Wallet Tracker and add a label such as Dev, Whale, or Insider.
  3. Rugscope checks recent pool trades for that wallet and adds a Rugscope badge beside matching wallet text when that wallet appears on the page.
  4. When Rugscope sees a new matching trade while the extension is running, it shows a Chrome notification.

Page markers

Page markers are compact Rugscope badges beside matching wallet text on the site you are viewing. Green means a buy match, red means a sell match, purple means a tracked wallet, and pink means the token creator/dev wallet. The tracked trades list in the panel shows the amount, USD volume, time, and transaction link when available.

Wallet alert history

When a tracked wallet triggers an alert, Rugscope saves it locally. Open Wallet Alerts from the extension popup to review the wallet label, buy or sell side, amount, USD volume, token, time, and transaction link. You can refresh the list or clear it when you no longer need the history.

Moving the panel

Drag the top of the Rugscope panel to move it. Use the lower-right handle to resize it. Rugscope saves the position and size locally, so the panel stays where you placed it during future scans.

Transparent 5-Pillar Risk Architecture

RugScope AI does not use arbitrary single-number formulas. It evaluates tokens against five objective on-chain pillars totaling 100 points:

1. Authorities & Permissions 30% Weight (Max 30 pts)

Audits active mintTo and freezeAccount keys, metadata mutability, and non-standard Token-2022 transfer fee extensions.

2. Liquidity Depth & Lock Security 25% Weight (Max 25 pts)

Scans for unlocked or burned LP tokens, micro-liquidity cushion (< $1K or < $10K), and severe FDV-to-liquidity exit ratios.

3. Holder Dispersal & Concentration 20% Weight (Max 20 pts)

Identifies insider monopolization. Flags top 10 supply concentration > 45% or > 70%, and single non-pool whale domination.

4. Market Dynamics & Volume Pressure 15% Weight (Max 15 pts)

Evaluates heavy sell-to-buy transaction pressure (> 2.5x sell ratio), acute 1h/24h drawdowns, and rapid liquidity extraction.

5. Maturity & Program Integrity 10% Weight (Max 10 pts)

Checks pool creation age, bonding curve graduation status, and verified DEX router programs (Raydium, Orca, Meteora).

Grounded AI Forensic Synthesis

Unlike generative text models that risk hallucinating non-existent blockchain transactions, RugScope's AI engine is strictly deterministic and evidence-bound. It directly ingests verified on-chain metrics from DexScreener, RugCheck, and Solana RPC, translating raw parameters into natural English summaries citing exact percentages, liquidity depth, and authority statuses with transparent ethical disclaimers.

RPC Fast Gateway & APIs

RugScope provides a secure, server-side gateway to interact with Solana RPC and public telemetry:

POST /api/rpc

Proxies Solana JSON-RPC requests (such as getSignaturesForAddress and getAccountInfo). When configured, routes through RPC Fast (RPC_FAST_URL, RPC_FAST_API_KEY) without leaking credentials to client browsers.

GET /api/status

Reports gateway health, active RPC provider (RPC Fast vs Solana Public Mainnet), and configuration state.

GET /api/proxy?url=...

Whitelisted CORS proxy for DexScreener and RugCheck REST endpoints with user-agent attribution.

Understanding the Score Scale

0–24 Low Risk Revoked authorities, verified locked liquidity, broad holder dispersal.
25–49 Moderate Risk Fair launch meme dynamics, elevated volatility, or nascent pool age.
50–74 High Risk Thin liquidity, heavy sell-side skew, or concentrated insider clusters.
75–100 Critical Threat Active freeze or mint keys, unlocked micro-liquidity, impending capital loss.

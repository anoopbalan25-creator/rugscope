RugScope 5-Pillar Risk Scoring Methodology

Transparent, math-backed security scoring with zero fake precision

RugScope aggregates verified on-chain indicators into five structured pillars totaling 100 points. A higher score reflects greater structural capital risk. The model avoids arbitrary fake precision and transparently details point deductions.

1. Authorities & Permissions Max 30 pts (30%)

Scans for active mintTo authority (+16), active freezeAccount authority (+14), mutable metadata, and non-standard Token-2022 transfer fee / permanent delegate extensions.

2. Liquidity Depth & Lock Security Max 25 pts (25%)

Evaluates unlocked or burned LP status (0–20% locked = +12 to +16), micro-liquidity cushion (< $1K = +16, < $10K = +10), and severe FDV-to-liquidity exit imbalances.

3. Holder Dispersal & Concentration Max 20 pts (20%)

Quantifies supply monopolization. Top 10 wallets holding > 70% supply add +14 pts (> 45% adds +8). Single whale accounts outside pool contracts add further dump exposure.

4. Market Dynamics & Volume Pressure Max 15 pts (15%)

Monitors heavy sell-side transaction skews (sells >= 2.5x buys adds +7), sudden 1-hour drawdowns (> 35% adds +8), and rapid liquidity depletion velocity.

5. Maturity & Program Integrity Max 10 pts (10%)

Factors in pair creation timestamp (< 1 hour adds +6), bonding curve migration status, and audited DEX router verification.
